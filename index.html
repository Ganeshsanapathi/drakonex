About DRAKONEX

DRAKONEX is a student-focused technology platform dedicated to bridging the gap between education and industry. We empower students through internships, real-world projects, AI innovation, mentorship, and career-focused learning experiences.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide students with practical experience, industry-relevant skills, and meaningful opportunities that prepare them for successful careers in technology.

🚀 Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted student-first technology platforms, helping millions of learners transform their skills into real-world impact and career success.
